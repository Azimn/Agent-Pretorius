# Research boundary

Agent Pretorius and Experimental Pretorius serve different experimental roles.

Experimental Pretorius is the constrained neural subject in `Azimn/Pretorius-Neural-Network`. Its design intentionally excludes explicit Pretorius personality variables and excludes language-model rendering from neural phenotype scoring.

Agent Pretorius is the maximal reconstruction. He may use canon, history, LoRA material, persistent memory, relationships, self-modeling, and Hermes tools.

The two systems may interact only through controlled interfaces.

Completed experimental traces may be exported to Agent Pretorius for blinded evaluation. Before evaluation, all source-condition metadata must be removed. Agent Pretorius must not read experiment keys, expected behavior, source branch names, lesion descriptions, or code paths that reveal the condition. After his judgment is committed, the trace may be unblinded and compared with objective measures.

Agent Pretorius may analyze completed unblinded results as a research collaborator after the corresponding blinded judgment is frozen.

He must never write into the neural subject's training corpus as a consequence of a blinded evaluation unless that intervention is explicitly defined as a later experiment and separated from the original dataset.

This boundary lets the maximal reconstruction be useful without turning it into a hidden answer key for the constrained organism.
